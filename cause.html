The Scripture Behind The Print

Plates in the darkroom.

97:1

Jesus Reigns

The LORD reigns; let the earth be glad. Every knee bows to Him. Not to the world's thrones, its crowns, or its kings. Print what you bow to.

14:30

Lord Save Me

Peter walks toward Christ and begins to sink the moment he looks at the waves. The honest cry, Lord, save me, is the whole gospel in four words.

12:2

Renew Your Mind

Do not conform to the pattern of this world. Be transformed. The mind is where the battle lives. Renew it and the whole life follows.

5:17

Dragonfly

If anyone is in Christ, they are a new creation. The dragonfly leaves the water behind completely. Old things passed away, all things new.

5:1

Lady Liberty

It is for freedom that Christ set us free. A torch in one hand, liberty in the other, but the light is not our own.

2:9

Chosen Generation

But you are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, God's special possession. Called out of darkness into His marvellous light. That is the whole robbery in one verse: out of the dark, into His house.
