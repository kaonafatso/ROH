The Name

ROH = Robbers of Hell. Not chosen for the sound of it. It is the job description: we bind the strong man and plunder his house, stealing back every soul he thought he owned.

The Two Pillars

I · DON'T DIE WHILE LIVING. Live intentionally. Explore. Create. Become. Wake up, then wake the next one.
II · EVERY KNEE SHALL BOW. Create freely. Live boldly. Explore endlessly. But remember who is greater. (Philippians 2:10)
