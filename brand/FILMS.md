# ROH / THE DARKROOM REELS
### 33 in-room film concepts + the 30-minute-a-day sustain plan
SEE DIFFERENT. LIVE PURPOSEFULLY.

---

## PART I — THE SYSTEM (every film obeys this)

1. **Format** — 9:16 vertical (TikTok / IG Reels / Shorts). 3–20 sec. 4K 24fps. Phone on tripod.
2. **Exposure** — never shoot pitch black. Underexpose but stay visible; the black comes from the grade.
3. **Grade (DaVinci Resolve, preset once)** — contrast up; shadows crushed to near-black; highlights pushed warm/cream (#b9975b); film grain 35mm at 25%; subtle vignette; very slight teal in shadows for depth. Save as a preset: one click on every reel.
4. **Sound** — whisper-VO recorded in a closet under a blanket (phone mic), OR text-on-screen only. Dark ambient loops: YouTube Audio Library, search "dark ambient no copyright".
5. **Text style** — every film opens with a BOLD STATEMENT (same font every time, e.g. Bebas Neue / Oswald, tracking up). Every film ends on the END CARD:
   - VERSE TAG
   - **SEE DIFFERENT. LIVE PURPOSEFULLY.**
   - `@ROH`
6. **Identity** — same intro treatment, same grade, same end card on every reel. That consistency IS the brand.

### Master in-room production recipe
- **Tripod** — chest height for sitting shots, floor height for flat-lay, face height for POV.
- **Light fallbacks (no ring light needed):**
  - Window light = key. Shoot at the window at golden hour or with a desk lamp as fill.
  - Desk lamp through a white t-shirt = soft diffused key.
  - Phone torch = perfect "torch / candle / flare" source and rim light.
  - Room light switches = instant dark/light cuts with a single switch.
- **Camera apps** — use manual lock: 4K24, shutter 1/48, ISO as low as possible, AE/AF locked (tap-and-hold on subject).
- **Stability trick** — shoot every shot twice: once mobile (hand-hands movement), once locked (gives you punch-in/zoom in edit).
- **DaVinci Fusion text template** — make ONE intro-text and ONE end-card template; reuse forever.
- **Batch shooting** — 90 minutes = 3 films. Set down the shot list (Part II), shoot all identical camera positions in a row, then move the tripod once.

## PART II — THE 33 CONCEPTS

### 1. JESUS REIGNS — Psalm 97:1
OPEN: "While the world begs for thrones, the earth is glad."

**A. "The Crown"** — Tripod: corner, chest height. Window light high.
1. Two hands drop a paper crown to the floor (4s).
2. Slow tilt up to a wooden chair as a throne, silhouette (6s).
3. Push-in on window flare, light blooms (5s).
4. End card. TAG: "The LORD reigns; let the earth be glad."

**B. "The Flag"** — Tripod: straight-on, floor height. Black bedsheet pinned flat = flag.
1. Black sheet fills frame (3s).
2. A single gold tape line is pinned across it (6s).
3. Slow pan down to the tee flat-lay on the bed (6s).
4. End card. TAG: "The LORD reigns; let the earth be glad."

**C. "The Mute"** — Screen recorded scrolling fast, apps glitching.
1. Screen-record scrolling, frantic (6s).
2. A hand covers the lens — cut (2s).
3. Candle in the dark, still (7s).
4. End card. TAG: "The LORD reigns; let the earth be glad."

## PART III — THE 30-MINUTE-A-DAY SUSTAIN PLAN

**Philosophy — bank before you broadcast.** A 90-minute Sunday block banks 3 films; the week is just wiring them out.

### Weekly loop (30 min / day)

| Day | The 30 minutes |
|---|---|
| **SUN** | SHOOT: film 1 concept from Part II (15) · snap b-roll / behind-the-scenes clips (10) · queue next concept (5) |
| **MON** | DISTRIBUTE: edit + grade + export with the preset (20) · post/schedule TikTok → IG Reels → Shorts (10) |
| **TUE** | COMMUNITY: read Wall embeds in Discord, reply, forward prayer requests (10) · bless 3–5 like-minded accounts (10) · queue 1 Wall quote-card (10) |
| **WED** | DISPATCH: draft 1 email in Buttondown (drop date or verse meaning) (15) · post 1 shareable quote card from the Wall (15) |
| **THU** | ORDERS: answer vault embeds, confirm sizes + payments by email (20) · note restock need (10) |
| **FRI** | PREP: shot-list the next 2 concepts from Part II (15) · tidy the shoot corner, check phone storage + battery (15) |
| **SAT** | PUBLISH WEEK: send Dispatch email · update wall.json + Discord · set next week's 3 targets (30) |

### Launch burst (weeks 1–2)
Two 90-minute blocks: bank **10+ films** first. Empty bank = dead feed; full bank = the movement runs itself on autopilot.

### Monthly cadence (one design drop per month)
Film that design (3 weeks) → Dispatch email ("the meaning behind the print") → vault sale → family watch in Discord → rotate to next design. Content mix per week: 1 reel · 1 quote card · 1 behind-the-scenes · 1 Wall highlight.

### KPIs (check every Saturday)
- Buttondown subscribers (goal: +5 / week early)
- Wall entries (goal: 3+ / week)
- Follower growth per platform
- Saves + shares per reel (goal: 2–3)

### Tools already live
Discord webhook (Wall + vault + Dispatch) · free Buttondown list · curated `js/wall.json` · the Vault. Most days above are 10-minute tasks, not content grind.

### Keep the loop honest
- One grade preset, one text template, one end card — no daily design work.
- Reuse b-roll across weeks (ten 20-sec shots = a month of content).
- If a day slips, the bank covers it. Never skip SAT without re-setting the week.
