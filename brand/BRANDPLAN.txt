ROH / ROBBERS OF HELL
========================
SEE DIFFERENT. LIVE PURPOSEFULLY.

THE NAME
------------------------
ROH = Robbers of Hell.
Not chosen for the sound of it. It is the job description.

We are the ones who bind the strong man and plunder his house,
stealing back every soul he thought he owned.
(Matthew 12:29 - then he can plunder his house.)

THE MOTTO
------------------------
TO ROB HELL.
That is the goal. Bind the strong man, plunder his house,
and carry every soul we can out of the dark.
Everything we make is a tool for that robbery.

THE TAGLINE
------------------------
SEE DIFFERENT. LIVE PURPOSEFULLY.
A creative movement rooted in faith, individuality, purpose,
and the courage to see beyond the ordinary.

THE TWO PILLARS
------------------------
I.  DON'T DIE WHILE LIVING.
    Live intentionally. Explore. Create. Become.
    Wake up, then wake the next one.

II. EVERY KNEE SHALL BOW.
    Create freely. Live boldly. Explore endlessly.
    But remember who is greater. (Philippians 2:10)

WHAT WE REPRESENT
------------------------
I.   FAITH          Believe beyond what is seen.
II.  PURPOSE        Don't simply exist.
III. INDIVIDUALITY  You weren't made to be a copy.
IV.  EXPLORATION    Go beyond the familiar.
V.   CREATIVITY     No boundaries to the imagined.
VI.  EVOLUTION      Become more than who you were.
VII. CONVICTION     Stand for something.
VIII.FREEDOM        Live without unnecessary limits.

THE VOICE
------------------------
Faith x streetwear x editorial. Dark, film-grainy, bold.
A door with no lock: everyone is welcome. No gatekeeping.
Community is open: prayers, testimonies, words of encouragement
land straight in the house Discord.

THE INTERACTIVE LANGUAGE (subtle, never loud)
------------------------
- Developing-room reveal: designs "develop" like film negatives as you scroll
- Scramble/decrypt headings that resolve like decoded scripture
- Verse drop-ins that appear as plates
- Film grain + vignette over the whole site
- Slow marquee of the pillars
- Magnetic buttons, ember cursor trail, flame scroll progress
- Near-invisible product tilt

POSITIONING
------------------------
ROH is not a merch brand with a church sticker on it.
ROH is robbery gear. Every print is a verse that pulls a soul
out of darkness. Bold, reverent, and open to everyone.

PHILOSOPHY OF FAITH / STYLE REFERENCES
------------------------
